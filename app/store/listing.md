**Full description (4000)**

Rocksane reads a bouldering wall and plans your route.

Point your phone at the wall or pick a photo, then tap one hold of your colour. Rocksane finds every hold of that colour, checks that each one really is a hold and not a painted patch of wall, and plans the hand and foot moves from the start to the top. A climber drawn at your real size shows each move from behind, the way you'd climb it.

WHAT YOU GET
• Every move, step by step: which hand or foot goes where, and how far.
• Coaching tips on each move: push with your legs, keep your arms straight, turn your hip in, match, flag, rest.
• Real size: Rocksane finds the floor and the top of the wall in your photo, so the climber has your height and reach. Drag the lines if a photo is unusual.
• Your body and your wall: set your height, arm span, the wall's height and its angle, from slab to overhang.
• Your edits: add a hold it missed, remove one that isn't part of the route, choose the start and the top.
• Learn to climb: a short guide to colour routes, footwork, body position, falling safely and the words you'll hear at the gym.
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking. Pictures never leave your phone.

ROCKSANE PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription)
• Live camera: point your phone at the wall and watch the moves appear.
• Video: record a clip and read the wall frame by frame.
• Hold type: mark jugs, crimps, slopers, pinches, pockets, sidepulls, gastons, underclings and volumes; the plan and the tips use them.
• All nine technique examples, animated step by step (jug, crimp and sloper are free).

Free forever: photos, the colour reader, real-size planning, the moves and the Learn guide.

SAFETY
Rocksane suggests moves, and it can miss holds or be wrong. Read every route yourself, follow your gym's rules and climb within your ability.
