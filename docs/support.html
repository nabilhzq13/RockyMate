Help with Rocksane on iPhone, Android and the web

It picked the wrong holds

Tap one hold of your route, ideally a big one in good light. Then use Add for a hold it missed and Remove for one that isn't part of the route. If the start or the finish is wrong, use Start and Top. A straight-on photo of the whole route works best.

The climber looks too big or too small

Rocksane draws the climber at your real size from where it finds the floor and the top of the wall. Tap Size and drag the two lines onto the floor (where the wall meets the mats) and the top of the wall. Set your height, arm span and the wall's height in Settings.

The camera doesn't start

  • iPhone: Settings → Apps → Rocksane → turn on Camera.
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Rocksane → Permissions → Camera → Allow.
  • Website: allow the camera when the browser asks. Browsers only allow it on https pages.

Restore Rocksane Pro

Open Settings in the app, then Rocksane Pro → Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with. Restoring needs an internet connection; everything else works offline.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle payments and refunds. On iPhone, ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund help.

Get in touch

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Say which phone you use and what you tapped, and add a screenshot if you can. Please don't post photos of other people.
