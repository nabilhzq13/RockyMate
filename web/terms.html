What Rocksane is

Rocksane reads the holds of one colour in a picture of a climbing wall and suggests hand and foot moves to the top, with tips and technique examples. It is a training aid, not a coach or an instructor, and it can be wrong.

Climbing safely

  • Climbing and bouldering are dangerous and can cause serious injury.
  • Rocksane can miss holds, mistake the wall for a hold, misjudge size and distance, or suggest moves that are too hard or unsafe for you.
  • Read the route yourself before you climb, climb within your ability, fall onto the mats, and follow your gym's rules and staff.
  • Don't use your phone while you climb, and keep it off the mats.

You climb at your own risk.
